In the queue

How it works

  1. On your phone A reel is worth keeping: tap « + Add a reel » and paste the link. From a theme page, the theme is noted. The link waits in the queue.
  2. Every few days, on your computer Chrome open with the Claude in Chrome extension, then in Claude Code: /exercise-reel. Any folder works once the skill is installed.
  3. While it runs Keep the computer unlocked and the reel tab in front: the video does not load in the background. Expect 10 to 15 minutes per sheet, more for a session.

Skill, templates and setup guide: the Reelbook repository. Notes are taken inside each sheet.

Sheets are built from reels for personal use. Every sheet credits its source.
