Set up Reelbook.

Under the hood, a Claude Code /skill turns your technique videos into a training notebook.

Before you start: Claude Pro with Claude Code, Chrome with the Claude in Chrome extension, Git and Python 3.9+. Then three things to do once: your account, the skill on your computer, the app on your phone.

Reelbook is open source. The skill, the app and the server are public on GitHub, under the MIT licence: you can read what you install, and host it yourself.See the code on GitHub ↗

How it works

  1. On your phone A video is worth keeping: tap « + Add a video » and paste the link. From a notebook page, the notebook is noted. It shows under On the way.
  2. Every few days, on your computer Chrome open with the Claude in Chrome extension, then in Claude Code: /reelbook. Any folder works once the skill is installed. On a Mac, tools/nightly.sh install runs it every night instead.
  3. While it runs Keep the computer unlocked and the video tab in front: the video does not load in the background.

Welcome to Reelbook

Three more steps. You only do this once.

    How it works

    On your phone, send

    A technique catches your eye in your feed: Share, Reelbook. The video waits in the queue. That’s all the phone does.

    On your computer, run

    Type /reelbook in Claude Code whenever you like. Keep Chrome open in the foreground, signed in to Instagram, TikTok or Facebook if your videos come from there (YouTube needs nothing). Claude watches each video and writes the sheet: photos, why, key points, the steps in order. On a Mac it can also run every night: tools/nightly.sh install.

    When you train, open the sheet

    Move a sheet to another group, add your notes, build a session from several notebooks. On the mat, open the sheet: the move first, in pictures. Tap Log.
