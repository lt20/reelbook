Use case: logo-brand.
Create a polished, carefully art-directed visual proposal for the app brand "Reelbook". It transforms saved how-to short videos into useful illustrated notebook pages. The user wants a distinctive real logo with personality, replacing a generic play button.
ONE coherent logo concept, not several competing logos. The concept is "Le R plié": a custom uppercase R built from a broad continuous folded ribbon/page. Make the silhouette beautifully balanced and immediately readable as R, bold and compact. Its upper bowl is gently squared and rounded, its diagonal lower leg feels like a folded strip of paper. Clever generous negative space hints at motion and a page, but the R must remain the primary readable shape. Not a stock open book, not a generic play triangle, not a film reel, no camera, no sparkles, no extra symbols. Masterful geometric minimalism with a tiny unexpected folded corner; strong recognizable silhouette at 24px. Mostly single color, optional restrained coral fold detail, no dependence on shading.
Palette: rich cobalt blue derived from #1f4fa3, warm ivory #faf8f2, restrained brick coral #c54b38. Flat vector-like shapes with crisply controlled geometry, no gradients, no 3D, no textures, no glossy mockups.
Present as a sophisticated landscape brand proposal board on warm ivory. Generous whitespace, clear hierarchy. Large main horizontal logo at upper left: bespoke R symbol plus exact wordmark "reelbook" in a refined contemporary bold lowercase sans, slightly compact spacing, warm and confident, not bubbly. Top small label exact text "REELBOOK / PROPOSITION 01". Under main logo small exact text "Le R plié". On the right show ONE large iOS-like rounded square app icon in cobalt containing the SAME ivory R mark, small coral fold detail if helpful. Lower band separated by a subtle hairline: show SAME mark in monochrome cobalt on ivory and inverse ivory on cobalt; two modest tiny app icon size previews illustrating legibility. A very short French line at bottom, exactly "De la vidéo au carnet." Every repeated mark must be identical in design. Avoid excessive labels, filler text, checkerboards, ornament, complex thin strokes. Make the actual logo and icon the focus. This is a credible professional brand identity proposal, not an infographic.
