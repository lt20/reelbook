## What
A device that has never signed in (no `rb.backend` stored) opens on a presentation inside the auth screen:
« Your videos, as sheets », a one-sentence summary, three steps with drawn SVG illustrations (paste a link →
Claude on your computer makes the sheet → read it during the workout), **What you need** (Claude Pro or above with
Claude Code, Chrome with Claude in Chrome, a computer that stays on), then « Create an account » (straight to the
hosted sign-up form) and « Sign in ». « Use my own Supabase » is now an advanced link under the form, in both
modes (sign-up included: the project is kept only once the account exists, as from the setup screen).
A returning device (`rb.backend` stored) goes straight to Sign in, as before. « See a demo » is in the markup,
hidden until the demo view exists (`views.includes('demo')`, added by L27).

## Why
Audit item O1: a visitor met a sign-in form and a backend choice before knowing what Reelbook is.

## Decisions
- The presentation lives inside `#auth` (above the title and form, which it hides) so `#auth` stays the view
  and the existing flows are untouched; the form's own « Create an account » toggle still opens the backend
  picker (`#setup`), which `tests/auth-flow.test.cjs` covers.
- `route()` now also writes the auth title (the acceptance test reads it on a returning device).
- « Use Reelbook hosted » shows in sign-up mode too when an own project is selected (it used to be sign-in only).
- No price in any text; the hosted blurb stays `app/config.js`'s.

## Pitfalls
- L27's branch adds its own « See a demo » link under `#auth-title`: when both are merged, keep one (this one is
  in the presentation; L27's also serves returning devices). The L25 acceptance test « stays hidden until L27 is
  merged » will turn red once L27's `demo` view is in `views`: expected, Codex to update it then.
