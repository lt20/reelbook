## What
`#welcome` is the mockup's list of steps: computer, phone, first sheet, the first one not done put forward. A banner
« Account created for x » on the first arrival, a title that follows the steps left, « Open my notebooks » right under
the steps, the guide collapsed, the landing's FR / EN switch on the page. On a phone or a tablet the computer step gives
no command: it says why and sends the line. On a computer it gives the line, the system detected, the other system, and
the phone step shows a QR (hosted address only). `rb.py login` writes `reelbook_cli_at` in `user_metadata`, so the
computer step ticks itself when the person comes back.

## Why
Decision 25 and the « Mockup review » of `docs/audit-design/P7-signup.md`: show as done only what the app really knows.

## Decisions
- « Account created » also needs `created_at` under 24 h and no sheet: a device opening an old empty account has not just
  created it. No date, no banner.
- Home-screen mode counts as the phone step on a phone or a tablet only: an installed app window on a Mac is not a phone.
- A request a computer took counts with `processed_at` set (`processing`, `error`), besides the latest `done`: both are
  written by `rb.py` only, and a retry sets it back to null.
- The steps are redrawn only when their html changed, so a focus read does not undo « Copied » or a selection.
- `mark_cli_login` calls `urllib` directly with a 10 s timeout, not `http()` (60 s, `die` on an HTTP error).

## Pitfalls
- `body:has(#intro:not([hidden]))` takes the page's padding and dark ground: a preview run without `rb.backend` in
  localStorage shows every signed-in page clipped. Real flows always have it; set it in the captures.
- Playwright's `check()` fails on `#w-tick`: the box is gone once ticked. Use `click()`.
- `#welcome` opens from Settings' « Full guide » and the home's « Setup guide for a new computer » too: for an account
  that has a sheet, the computer step says « Linked » and points to Settings for the line.
