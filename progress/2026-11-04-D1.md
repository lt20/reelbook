## What
- Edit on a session is written only over the version it was opened on (`updated_at` in the write's condition). If
  the session changed since (republished by `rb.py`, saved from another tab), nothing is written, the edits stay on
  screen and « Reload » opens the new version in Edit. Deleted meanwhile: back to the notebook.
- Sign out (and « another backend ») saves the note being typed, waits for the account's note writes (5 s at most),
  then deletes the account's note drafts; a draft still not stored is deleted only after a confirmation.

## Why
COORDINATION « Debt »: #36 (a tab left open overwrote structured doses published since) and #30 (drafts kept on the
device after sign-out, against decision 8).

## Decisions
- `sessions.updated_at` exists since the table was created (#14): read with the catalogue, no migration. A session
  from a catalogue cached before this deploy has no version: written without the condition, as before.
- Conditional write (`eq updated_at`) rather than a read before writing: no window between check and write.
- Reload opens the new version (not a merge); the user copies their edits before pressing it.
- Unsaved draft at sign-out: `window.confirm` (No keeps the account signed in and the draft); a revoked session keeps
  its drafts (no sign-out was asked on this device).
- During sign-out the note field is read-only (given back on No). Drafts are deleted only once `signOut()` succeeded:
  refused (offline), Sign out keeps the session and the drafts; « another backend » goes on (the user agreed, and an
  unreachable backend is a reason to switch). A draft that appears during the sign-out (another tab) is asked about
  again, never dropped silently (Codex passes 2 and 3).
- Theme move and Add to session store the `updated_at` their write returns, so a later Edit is not refused.

## Pitfalls
- The harness ran every timer at once: the 5 s cap of the sign-out wait won over the note write. New harness option
  `timerCap` (timers of that many ms or more never fire); `confirm` and `localStorage.key/length` added too.
- The worktree guard refuses `for f in app/*.js; node --check $f`: one `node --check` per file.
