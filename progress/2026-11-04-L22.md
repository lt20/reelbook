## What
The headings the app draws inside a session follow the session's language: a French session reads Pourquoi,
Points clés, Format, Exercices, Dose, Repos, Séries; its content blocks carry `lang="fr"`. Buttons, menus, hints,
« Session done », Save to log and Close stay English.

## Why
Audit O6, decision 2 (3 Nov): the interface stays English, content headings follow the content.

## Decisions
- `RS.isFrench` holds the L1 rule (three distinct common French words); `sheetLang` and `RS.sessionLang` share it.
- `lang="fr"` goes on content blocks only (title, format pill, Why / Key points, the Exercises heading, the player's
  dose area); interface controls inside a French block get `lang="en"` (rest buttons, set boxes).
- A visible `Dose` label above the player's big dose (all sessions, symmetric with `Rest`) and a `Format` label in
  the session's format pill: Codex's tests ask for them. On the session page « Dose » is visually hidden (`.vh`).
- The end-screen summary stays English (it prefills the practice log).

## Pitfalls
- Codex's tests match `<h2>Exercices` and `<h2>Pourquoi</h2>` with no attribute on the `h2`: the `lang` goes on a
  wrapping `div`, not on the heading.
- The preview fixtures hold French sessions only; the English screenshots used a session injected into the
  scratchpad preview's `vendor/fake-data.js`.
