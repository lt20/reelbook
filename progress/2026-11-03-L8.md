## What
Home: « To try » after the « New » row, before the search box and the notebooks. Up to 3 sheets added more than
3 days ago and never practised, a new choice each day. « Not for me » drops a sheet on this device. Hidden
without the practice log or when nothing qualifies.

## Why
Audit item B3: bring back the sheets saved and never tried, without streaks or reminders.

## Decisions
- Pure `RS.toTry` in `app/sessions.js`, ranked by FNV-1a of `day|slug` (local date). `rb.totry.<user id>`
  ({day, slugs}, the shelf last shown) keeps the day's cards in place on « Not for me » or reload, and pushes them
  to the back the next day (never the same sheets two days running when more than 3 qualify). Codex passes 2 and 3.
- Home left open past midnight: re-render on visibilitychange and every minute (as L6). Codex pass 1.
- « More than 3 days » in calendar days (consistent with the daily seed; an hourly count lets « exactly 3 days »
  through by milliseconds).
- `rb.dismissed.<user id>` (coordinator decision), trimmed to the sheets that still exist on each dismissal.
- Reuses L6's `practiceOk` / `lastDone`: no second read of `practice_log`.

## Pitfalls
- The acceptance case `empty catalogue` expects `#home`; the app sends a first-time user with no sheet to
  `#welcome`. Raised under Open questions.
- Bash tool in a worktree refuses loops and variables around commands: one plain command per call.
