## What
- `supabase/schema.sql`, new section « Daily alert (L33) » at the end: `beta_alert_message()` builds the day's line
  (counts only), `beta_alert(at_8_only)` posts it to ntfy through pg_net with the topic read from Vault
  (`reelbook_ntfy_topic`), and a guarded `do` block schedules `reelbook-daily-alert` (`0 6,7 * * *` UTC, sends at
  the run that is 08:00 in Zurich) only when `pg_cron` and `pg_net` are both enabled.

## Why
Decision 17 (daily alert by the database itself, 70 % / 90 %); coordinator's arbitration on PR #74 (L32 « To
confirm » 1: the app should be able to say « come back tomorrow »).

## Decisions
- Topic in Vault (Supabase's recommended secret store; on every hosted project), not a `beta_settings` column: the
  alert then works before the settings row exists (the row comes only once L31 is live).
- Two UTC runs (06:00, 07:00) and an hour check in `Europe/Zurich`: 08:00 Zurich all year, one send a day.
- Per-day figures: today so far (UTC) as in the spec, plus all of yesterday (at 07:00 UTC « today » covers 6–7 hours,
  so a near-cap day shows in the next morning's line); thresholds read both.
- Waiting list « new since yesterday »: `beta_daily.waitlist` of yesterday and today (UTC days).
- Without the settings row: a « beta off » line with counts and no limits / tag.
- A limit of 0 (a deliberate closure) is left out of the ratio.
- Without a topic, Vault or pg_net: `beta_alert` raises (visible in the cron runs and in the SQL editor) rather than
  doing nothing in silence.
- The two functions are created everywhere (inert, closed to clients); only the schedule is guarded, and it needs
  pg_cron, pg_net and supabase_vault (Codex local review: without Vault the job could never send).
- Videos: the higher of the `requests` rows and `beta_daily` (which only counts once the settings row exists); only on
  the day the row is added, with links before it and deletes after it, can it read low (accepted, in the PR).
- L32 line changed (Codex pass 2): `beta_on_request` reads the settings after the `links` lock.
