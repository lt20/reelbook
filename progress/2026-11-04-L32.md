## What
- New section « Beta locks (L32) » at the end of `supabase/schema.sql`, off until a row of `beta_settings` exists.
  With the row (Laurent's values 50 / 14 / 10 / 10 GB / 300): sign-ups refused when closed, full
  (`seats_offset + accounts >= seats_total`) or past 10 today (UTC); new links refused past 300 a day for the whole
  project; photos refused once the sheets bucket holds 10 GB; three kill switches (sign-ups, links, photos).
- Exempt accounts: an `account_quotas` row whose note starts with `exempt` takes no seat and passes locks 3 and 4.
- `beta_seats()` (anon): `{taken, total, open}`, or null without the row.
- `beta_waitlist` (RLS on, no policy, no grant) and `join_waitlist(first_name, email, lang, consent)` (anon): only
  while full or closed, consent required, 200 new rows a day, the same `ok` for a listed email. An account that
  confirms an email deletes the row of that email.
- `docs/privacy.md` (draft): « Waiting list » paragraph. README: the two tables, and the beta being off without a row.

## Why
Decision 15 (50 seats, 14 counted from the start, 10 a day, 10 GB, 300 videos a day) and decision 20 (waiting list):
bound the whole hosted project so a successful beta cannot run up a bill. L29 bounds one account only.

## Decisions
- Laurent (4 Nov, via the coordinator): 14 is an offset inside the 50 (full after 36 real sign-ups); the consent
  sentence is validated; the privacy paragraph sits in the draft note without its own flag; no automatic email.
- `open` in `beta_seats()` = `signups_open` and a seat left; the daily cap is not in it (a busy day is not « full »,
  the waiting list stays for a full or closed beta). `join_waitlist` accepts in the same cases (full, or closed).
- Default `seats_offset` is 0 (an own backend that adds a row gets no phantom seats); Laurent's row says 14.
- Links and photos of exempt accounts still count in the project totals (they cost the same); exempt accounts are
  never refused by them.
- Kill switches on links and photos raise `beta: paused`; the photo cap raises `beta: project limit of photos`
  (the spec gave no message for these; both start like the ones L31 maps).
- An account takes a seat (and a slot of the day) once confirmed, or while pending for an hour (Supabase's default
  link lifetime): sign-ups with addresses nobody confirms do not block the beta (Codex pass 1). One confirmed later
  by « resend » may take a seat past the total: the trigger only checks the insert.
- The waiting-list row goes when an account confirms that email (insert already confirmed, confirmation, confirmed
  email change), not at an unconfirmed sign-up: anybody can type someone else's address (Codex pass 1).
- Photos: no project-wide lock (uploads would all queue); parallel uploads may pass 10 GB by a few files of 5 MB.
  Links: project-wide advisory lock, exact.
- `photo_quota_ok` calls the beta check only if `beta_upload_ok(uuid)` exists (`to_regprocedure`): a run that
  stops halfway never breaks uploads.
- Waiting list: the daily cap answers before the lookup, so a refusal tells nothing about who is listed. An invalid
  `lang` is stored as null rather than refused.

## Pitfalls
- Supabase Auth wraps any insert error as « Database error saving new user » (HTTP 500,
  `apierrors.NewInternalServerError(...)` in `internal/api/signup.go`): the `beta: …` message reaches the Auth logs,
  not the client. The app must read `beta_seats()` before a sign-up (L31).
- `tests/quota-schema.test.cjs` read from the quota section to the end of the file: its slice now stops at the beta
  section (it asserts the exact function list and « no delete »).
- `requests_quota` freezes `created_at` on update: to move links to yesterday in a test, use
  `session_replication_role = replica`.
- The worktree sandbox refuses long heredocs into the repo: use Edit / Write, or a script in the scratchpad.
