## What
- **Home « New » row** (B2): above the search and the notebooks, after the queue, a horizontally scrolling row of
  at most 6 cards, newest first: sheets whose `added_at` and video sessions whose `created_at` are within 72 h, not
  opened yet on this device. Opening a sheet or a session page adds its key to `localStorage` `rb.seen` (JSON
  array, sheet slug or `session/<slug>` as `RS.noteKey`, last 500). No row when empty, while loading, on a load
  error, during a search, on a notebook page.
- **Night run notice** (B2): `tools/nightly.sh` takes the time before Claude starts and, after the run, asks
  `rb.py list --since <iso>`; the ntfy notice reads « 2 new sheets: Curl marteau en 5 étapes, Backroll » (plus
  « · N still queued » when some are left, « (claude exit N) » on a failure, « Night run: nothing published … »
  when empty) with a `Click:` header to `REELBOOK_APP_URL` (default `https://lt20.github.io/reelbook/`), `#<slug>`
  (or `#session/<slug>`) when exactly one. The text is built by the pure function `night_notice`, tested from
  Python (the script can now be sourced).
- **`rb.py`**: `list --since <iso>` (sheets by `added_at`, sessions by `created_at`, oldest first, a new session's
  exercise sheets folded into it); `queue` returns `note`; `requeue-stuck` keeps a « Redo: » note instead of
  overwriting it with « requeued after a run stopped mid-way ».
- **« Something wrong? »** (B4): a text button in its own block right under « My notes » on a sheet or session page
  that has a source URL; its dialog « What should Claude fix? » + « Send » (disabled while blank) inserts
  `{url: source.url, theme: <the page's notebook>, note: 'Redo: <text>'}` and shows « Sent. Claude will redo this
  sheet on the next run. » (Cancel becomes Close). A failure keeps the dialog and the text, with « Could not send
  it right now. Try again in a moment. ».
- Queue rows whose note starts with « Redo: » show a « Redo » pill before the URL.
- `skill/reelbook/SKILL.md`, queue reading only (§1): a « Redo: » request is a correction, same slug (session: same
  session and exercise slugs), keep its notebook and group, start from `work/<slug>/`, list it in the end-of-run
  report.

## Why
Audit B2 (a delivered sheet goes unnoticed: nothing on the home page, a night notice that only counts) and B4 (no
way to say a sheet is wrong from the phone).

## Decisions
- The « New » row sits after the queue and before the search (spec: « above the notebooks »).
- A new video session stands for its exercise sheets, in the row and in the night notice (otherwise one session
  of 8 exercises fills the row and the notice). User sessions are not « New » (the spec says video sessions).
- The row's content and the dialog's content are written by JS (the harness only sees `innerHTML` set by the app).
- Night notice wording beyond the spec's example: « · N still queued », « (claude exit N) », « Night run: nothing
  published (claude exit N) »; on an `rb.py list` failure, the old counting notice without « before/after ».
- `Click:` only on the end-of-run notice (not on « signed out » / « on battery »).
- `--since` subtracts one minute from the Mac's clock (`date -v-1M`) for a backend clock slightly behind.
- Preview fixtures: three existing items made recent through « now-<N>h » stamps, resolved by `fake-data.py` at
  build (a fixed date would fall out of the 72 h window); no new sheet (its photos need Pillow, absent here).

- After Codex pass 1: a failed correction keeps its « Redo: » line first (`rb.py error`, error on the next line),
  the app shows the error and Retry puts the line back; the text is sent on one line; `rb.py queue` adds
  `redo_of` (sheets and sessions published from that URL); a sheet joins `rb.seen` only once shown.
- After pass 2: `list --since` asks for `origin=video` sessions only. After pass 3: `list --since` prints tab-separated lines, `night_notice` splits on tabs (a group named « Upper body session » made a `#session/…` link).
- Coordinator decision after Codex review ×3: the Redo insert carries `slug` (the page flagged; a session page
  `session/<slug>`, as `RS.noteKey`), in the existing `requests.slug` column (no migration). Spec §B4 updated
  first, then only the two payload `deepEqual` of `tests/acceptance/l7/report-correction.test.cjs` (separate
  commit). `rb.py queue` selects `slug`; the skill fixes that page first, the others are in `redo_of`.
- « To confirm » settled by the coordinator (3 Nov): a redone sheet keeps its `added_at` → OK; « New » after the
  queue, before the search → OK; the exercise sheets of a new video session shown only through its session card → OK.

## Pitfalls
- Merge with L5 (#30): conflicts on the session and sheet page lines, resolved as
  `notesHtml() + reportHtml(e) + seatNav() + del` (sheet) and `notesHtml() + reportHtml(s)` (session).
- A republished sheet keeps its `added_at` (upsert without the column): a redone sheet does not come back in « New »
  nor in the night notice list.
- `bash -c 'source file'` sets `$0` to the file: the source guard then runs the script; the test passes `bash` as
  `$0`.
