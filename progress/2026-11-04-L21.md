## What
- Each notebook has a cover: its colour as a tinted band and a pictogram drawn for Reelbook (`app/icons.svg`, 30
  symbols: one per library notebook, `custom` for a notebook an account described).
- Home cards open on the cover (pictogram on a paper disc, counter, title). The notebook page wraps its title in the
  same cover; scrolled under the sticky bar, the bar takes the band and the pictogram sits before the compact title.
- « Add a notebook » shows each pictogram beside its title.
- `app/config.js`: key `icon` only. Service worker: `icons.svg` in `SHELL`, `config.js?v=7`, `rb-shell-35`.

## Why
Audit item O8: the home page read like a list of titles; six notebooks share the same colour (water and board sports).

## Decisions
- Band = 14 % notebook colour over the paper; text on it = 60 % colour + 40 % ink, measured ≥ 5.8:1 for the 8 palette
  colours in light and dark; notebook page title stays in ink (L20). Pictogram in the pure colour (≥ 3.2:1).
- Icon names = notebook ids. Pictograms flagged « À confirmer » in the PR (barre = tutu, prenatal = pram, pelvic floor =
  target, mobility = circular arrows, beauty = mirror, custom = notebook).
- The 6 px top border of the cards and of the notebook eyebrow (L20) give way to the band.
- The compact bar icon lives in a new `#nav-mid` wrapper so `#nav-title` keeps only the title text (tests read it).

## Pitfalls
- `tests/acceptance/l13/home.test.cjs` matches `<a class="tcard" href="#…">…</a>` and counts its `<img>`: the
  pictogram is an inline `<svg><use>`, never an `<img>`, and the class stays exactly `tcard`.
- A `vm`-loaded config gives arrays from another realm: `deepEqual` needs a JSON round trip.
- A comment in `icons.svg` must not contain a literal `<symbol` or `href=` (the test parses symbols with a regex).
