## What
The home page opens on photos. A compact brand bar (28 px mark, 22 px « reelbook » wordmark, « + Add a video »,
« Settings », one line, no eyebrow) with the small counter under it; then search, « New », « To try », the collapsed
queue summary, the notebooks. Notebook cards show their three latest sheets as 4:5 thumbnails, with the sheet titles
as alt text; the card link carries an explicit `aria-label` (« Strength, 25 sheets, 3 sessions ») that VoiceOver
reads instead of the alt texts (coordinator, after Codex pass 1). « How it works » leaves the home page once the account has 5 sheets (it stays hidden while the
catalogue loads); the « Full guide » link in Settings stays. The « New » and « To try » rows use cards at 44 % of the
width (220 px at most), like the notebook rows of L12, and bleed to the screen edge; the « Not for me » buttons line up
under equal-height cards.

First photo thumbnail on an iPhone 14 viewport with the synthetic fixtures (Playwright, `getBoundingClientRect`):
**y = 185 px** (first « New » card); the first notebook thumbnail is at 1 220 px (798 px without the practice table).

## Why
Audit E3: the brand block and the queue filled the first screen; no exercise photo was visible.

## Decisions
- One `<header id="brand-bar">` serves the home page and the notebook pages (the app draws both in `#home`). On a
  notebook page (`#home.nb`) the title goes back to its large display size on its own line, without the mark;
  « + Add a video » and « Settings » sit under it; the crumb and the eyebrow « Find it fast, when you need it » stay
  above the bar. The home eyebrow « Your videos, by notebook » is gone (spec: no eyebrow above the logo).
- The wordmark is lowercase « reelbook » as on the sign-in page; the `<h1>` text stays « Reelbook ».
- Under 360 px wide the bar's gaps and paddings tighten so it stays on one line at 320 px (measured: bar 44 px tall,
  wordmark not truncated; Codex pass 1 remark).
- Preview tooling: `?empty=all` (or a list of tables) in the fake backend, for the empty-account screenshot.
- Dead CSS removed with the old header: `.head`, `h1.brand-title`. `.topbar` stays (no markup uses it any more,
  but `tests/acceptance/l4/polish.test.cjs` reads its rule).

## Pitfalls
- The acceptance test reads the first CSS rule that starts with `#brand-bar{`, `#brand-wordmark{`, `#count{` and
  `.tcard .thumbs img{`: the `#home.nb …` overrides come after the base rules, and the thumbnail ratio was changed in
  place, not overridden in the L13 block.
