## What
- Home queue folds into one summary row « 3 in the queue · 1 failed » (button, chevron, `aria-expanded`), closed
  by default, open state kept in `localStorage` `rb.queueOpen`. Empty queue: no row.
- Rows: « Waiting » (neutral), « Processing » with « since HH:MM » (from `processed_at`), « Failed » (danger) with
  the reason on its own line and « Retry » / « Remove ». Retry resets the row (`status queued`, `note` and
  `processed_at` null); on failure the row stays and shows « Retry failed: <Supabase message> ». A queued request
  older than 7 days adds « waiting N days ».
- Expanded queue ends with the computer status: « Last run: today / yesterday / N days ago » (latest `done`
  request), or « No computer has processed a video yet. Set up your computer » (`#welcome`).
- Removing a row is deferred (a failed delete puts the row back with « Could not remove it: <reason> »): the row goes, a « Removed · Undo » toast with a 5 s bar; the delete is sent when the
  bar ends, on `pagehide`, when the page is hidden, or when another row is removed; Undo puts the row back. The
  DELETE is sent with `keepalive` (fetch wrapper given to supabase-js) and the id stays out of reads until it answers.
- `--danger` / `--danger-bg` tokens (light `#b42318`, dark `#f97066`); « Delete sheet » / « Delete session »
  (dialog buttons and the openers) are danger text buttons.
- 44 px tap targets: `.btn.sm`, `.mv`, top-bar and breadcrumb links, « Settings », « + New session », queue
  buttons (negative margins on the links, so the layout does not move). Pressed state: no tap highlight,
  opacity .6 on buttons and links, scale .97 on cards.
- Session cards: the format sentence is a muted two-line `.card .fmt`; pills stay on one line with an ellipsis.

## Why
Design audit items Q6 (queue states unreadable, no retry, no sign the computer runs), Q7 (small tap targets,
« danger » in the warning colour, immediate delete) and Q8 (format sentence as a 5-line pill).

## Decisions
- Failed count: a danger dot before the label, the label itself in ink (the acceptance test needs the text in one
  run). Open question in COORDINATION.md.
- Toast timer driven by the bar's `animationend`, not `setTimeout` (the harness runs timers at once). Open question.
  Reduced motion: the bar does not shrink but the 5 s still run.
- Retry/Remove of a failed row are text buttons under the reason; other rows keep the × remove button.
- The note of a non-failed row is no longer shown (the spec ties the reason to « Failed »).
- Preview fixtures: `processed_at` on the processing request, a `done` request (Last run), a longer session
  format (to show the 2-line clamp). `make-fixtures.py` updated by hand to match (no Pillow here).
- `tests/e2e/steps/smoke.json` 02-home: waits for `#portal >> text=Strength` and the summary « 1 failed »
  (the old « needs a look » label is gone, and the first « Strength » match was a hidden queue row).
- `tests/auth-flow.test.cjs` C2: the failed label is now « Failed ».

## Pitfalls
- Playwright `waitForSelector('#home >> text=X')` waits for the *first* match to be visible: a folded queue hides
  rows that contain notebook names.
- The 44 px test reads the *first* CSS rule of each selector: change the original rule, not an override below.
