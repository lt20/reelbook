## What
Nothing new on screen. Each session item now carries numbers for the coming session player (L10, L11): `sets`,
`reps`, `seconds`, `rest_s`, `per_side`. `/reelbook` may write them on an item when the video states them; otherwise
the app reads them from the free-text dose (`RS.parseDose`) and the rest from the session format (`RS.parseRest`).
The free-text dose stays what the user reads and edits.

## Why
Audit item S2: the player needs sets, reps, time and rest as numbers; today they are free text.

## Decisions
- Parser, measured on the corpus (fixtures + Laurent's 12 sessions in `work/`, 3 Nov): 23/25 nonempty distinct doses
  read; 7/13 formats state a rest and all 7 are read, the 6 others give null (they state none).
- The dose is cut at « · » and « ; », and the first part that starts with a dose counts; « suggestion : » is skipped.
  So « 3 × 8 · 4 s ↓ » gives 3 × 8 (the tempo is not a time), « finition · suggestion : 30 s par côté » gives
  30 s per side.
- The three composite doses (Codex's note): « avant la séance · suggestion : 30 s par position et par côté » →
  seconds 30, per side (the simplest reading of its second part); « suggestion : 3 × 5 à 10 pompes, puis 3 × 20 s au
  mur » → all null (two set schemes in one dose); « à-coups de 10 » → all null (does not start with a dose).
- Ranges: the upper bound counts (`reps`, `seconds`, rest), `repsMin` keeps the lower one for reps; « - », « – »,
  « à », « to ».
- A bare number is not read as reps unless a reps word or a side follows (« 10 squats » stays text).
- `parseRest`: a stated duration wins over « no rest » (« no rest between exercises, 90 s rest between rounds » →
  90); « sans pause » is not a rest.
- Structured fields win field by field (an item with only `reps` still gets `sets` from its dose); a value that is
  not a whole number ≥ 0 (or a boolean for `per_side`) is ignored by the app and refused by `rb.py publish`
  (null = absent). `rest_s` falls back to `parseRest(session.format)`.
- Edit on a session: an untouched dose keeps its structured fields; an edited dose drops them, so the numbers then
  come from the text the user wrote (before this lot, Edit saved `{slug, dose}` only and would have erased them).
- `sessions.js?v=6` and `rb-shell-26`: L8 (#34) takes 5 / 25, so both lots can merge in either order without
  sharing a version.

## Pitfalls
- No regex lookbehind in app modules: iOS Safari < 16.4 throws on the whole file (Codex, pass 1);
  `tests/dose.test.cjs` guards `sessions.js` and `sheet.js`.
- Three L9 acceptance tests are wrong as written (count 6 vs 7, a `None` path in the publish stub, `#home` hidden on
  a notebook route): detailed in COORDINATION.md, Open questions.
- `tests/rb-*.test.py` do not run under `unittest discover` (the dash in the name): run each file, as CI does.
