## What
Search reads the text of the sheets and the user's notes. « tapis » now finds the sheets that mention a mat in
their body (39 of Laurent's 107 sheets, 0 before), « dos » no longer finds « abdos » (match at word start, accents
and case ignored). A card found through the sheet text or the note says « in the sheet » / « in your note » under
its title; cards found by their metadata come first. Empty state: « Nothing matches « x ». Try a body part or a
piece of kit. » (« … » in this notebook » on a notebook page).

- Schema: `sheets.search_text text not null default ''` (appended, idempotent). `db push` by Laurent.
- `rb.py publish` writes it (visible text, kit pills first, whitespace collapsed, ≤ 4 000 characters cut at a
  word; not the `<title>`, scripts, styles, image alt texts). On a backend without the column the sheet is still
  published, without it, with a warning.
- `rb.py backfill-search [--dry-run]`: fills the sheets of the signed-in account whose `search_text` is empty,
  50 rows a read, keyset on slug. Refuses with a clear message before the column exists. Never run by the agent.
- App: the catalogue selects `search_text`; a backend without it answers 42703 once and the sheets are read as
  before (no error), until the next start. Notes are read with their body (`slug,body`). Both are kept in the
  cached catalogue (IndexedDB), so search works offline on the last catalogue.

## Why
Audit item E4: search only read titles, summaries, groups, notebooks and authors.

## Decisions
- Kit pills go first in `search_text` so the cap never drops them; their text is not repeated.
- Note bodies are cached next to the L19 snapshot by `index.html` (`noteBodies` added around
  `RS.catalogueSnapshot`), so `sessions.js` is untouched (no `?v=` / `SHELL` / `VERSION` bump).
- A v2 session's note (`session/<slug>`) is searched too; sessions have no `search_text`.
- The « + New session » exercise filter keeps its substring match (not in this lot's zone).
- `rb.py publish` prints its « published … » line on stderr: the L14 acceptance test parses the last stdout line
  of `_publish_sheet` as JSON. Nothing parses that line (nightly.sh reads `rb.py list`).

## Measured
Catalogue with Laurent's 107 local sheets (`build.sh --work`, the columns the app selects, JSON): 53.7 KB without
`search_text`, 222.3 KB with (+168.5 KB; median 943 characters a sheet, 2 sheets reach the 4 000 cap). The cached
catalogue in IndexedDB grows by the same amount.

## Pitfalls
- The backfill is an UPDATE: the `sheets_touch` trigger bumps `updated_at` of every backfilled sheet. The photo
  URLs carry `updated_at` (L19 `rbv`), so the service worker downloads each card photo once more after the
  backfill. Harmless, one-off; avoidable only by changing the trigger (not additive).
- `tests/harness.cjs` grew `missing: ['table.column']` (42703) and `client.selects`; the preview grew
  `?missing=sheets.search_text`.
