## What
The landing (`#intro`) is the v3 mockup: /skill hero, « Tes activités, tes carnets » band, « Du reel au geste » with
five examples (MMA, Danse, Étirement, Snowboard, HIIT) and three steps, tablet + phone, sessions, progress, the skill,
installation with macOS-Linux / Windows tabs and the open-source band, day to day, close, footer. FR and EN word for
word. The beta state follows `beta_seats()`; at the limit, the waiting-list card and « Rejoindre la liste d’attente ».
`<head>` has a real title, description, canonical, Open Graph (1200×630 picture) and Twitter card.

## Why
Lot L36 (decisions 13, 19; Laurent 5-6 Nov), on top of L30's v2 (#75).

## Decisions
- **Port by converter, not by hand** (scratch script, not committed): the mockup's stylesheet and markup are scoped
  mechanically (classes and ids `lp-`, rules under `#intro`, keyframes `lp-*`), its FR markup and `EN` object become
  the dictionary, its examples' data is generated into `app/landing.js` between `@gen-start` and `@gen-end`.
  Transitions moved under `prefers-reduced-motion:no-preference` (the app's rule, `tests/l21-identity.test.cjs`).
  A new test compares every string with `docs/audit-design/mockups/p6-landing.html`, so the next copy change is
  « update the mockup copy, regenerate the block ».
- **Pictures**: frames 640 px, thumbnails 240 px (`-t`), q70, one folder per example + `yoga` and `notebooks`; L30's
  `assets/dance` and `assets/mma` (with its manifest mechanism) removed. Dead data of the mockup (items never shown:
  `osoto`, `fouette`, `heels`…; v2 search, notes, groups) dropped, so their pictures are not shipped.
- **Beta**: no answer = open without a count (no number invented). Full = `taken >= total` or `open: false`. No
  visitor switch, no `?places=` in the app; tests and captures call `RBL.seats()`.
- **Kept from L30 although the mockup does not show it**: on « Sur mon Supabase », step 2 replaces the hosted
  install lines with L30's sentence (the hosted line would tie the skill to the wrong backend); the visible
  « copy by hand » message.
- **Waiting list off until L31** (Codex pass 1: a form that records nothing is a dead end): `WAITLIST = false` in
  `landing.js`; a full beta shows « Bêta complète : 50 sur 50 » without the form, the hero line « Bêta complète »
  (the mockup's line cut before « · inscris-toi… »), the buttons « Créer mon carnet » to step 1, where « Sur mon
  Supabase » stays open. The form is in the markup, hidden, its button disabled; L31 flips the switch. `#shortcut` opens the
  sign-in form with `#settings` next (the shortcut needs an account). Stretch's Chrome bar `instagram.com/…`
  instead of the mockup's `instagram.com/reel/…` (DoD).

## Pitfalls
- The sandbox refuses shell loops and `cd` + variables in one command: write small Node scripts and run them.
- `sips -c` crops at the centre: only `--resampleWidth` here.
- Ports 8837/8838 were taken by another session; the mockup was served on 8946, the app on 8836.
- `deepEqual` on an array made inside the `vm` fails across realms: spread it first.
- Static `[hidden]` loses to a class's `display`: `#intro [hidden]{display:none!important}` is in the extras.
