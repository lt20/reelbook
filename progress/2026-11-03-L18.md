## What
Under 600 px wide every `dialog.dlg` is a bottom sheet: full width, top corners 14 px, safe-area bottom padding,
slide-up entry (none with reduced motion). Form dialogs (Move, Add to a session, New session, Something wrong?)
have a header « Cancel | title | action »; the bottom pair comes back from 600 px. Delete session / Delete sheet /
Not queued yet are action sheets (danger in red on top, the way out apart at the bottom, focused). No text field
is focused when a dialog opens. The sheet is bounded by the visual viewport (`--vvh`, `--kb`), is its only scroll
container, and keeps its header sticky. A backdrop tap closes a sheet with nothing typed or picked in it.

## Why
Audit item E8: centred dialogs put the actions far from the thumb, and in « New session » the keyboard hid the
list and the button.

## Decisions
- One helper `openSheet` / `closeSheet` (+ `sheetSaved`) inline in `index.html`, not a module: the harness runs only
  the inline script and `sessions.js`, and tests may not change. Guarded: no `showModal` / `close` → `open`
  attribute; no `visualViewport`; no `getBoundingClientRect` → a click on the dialog itself counts as backdrop.
- Header buttons move first in the DOM (iOS reading order); from 600 px `display:contents` + `order` puts them back
  at the bottom, primary first. All ids kept.
- « Keep it » label kept on the destructive sheets; backdrop tap closes at every width; any `input` event (select
  included) marks a sheet unsaved. Flagged « À confirmer » in the PR.
- `tests/e2e/shots.mjs`: optional per-step `viewport` (800 px wide, 380 px high for the keyboard).

## Pitfalls
- The base rule `.sh-h .sh-ok{order:1}` (for ≥ 600 px) also applies inside the mobile header grid: reset `order`
  under 600 px, or the title ends up left of both buttons.
- Sticky header in a scroll container with padding: `top:-20px` (the padding) with `margin-top:-20px`.
- The harness keeps one listener per event type per element: the helper's `click` / `input` listeners on a dialog
  are the only ones on it.
