## What
- A sticky bar on every signed-in page but home and the player: « ‹ <parent> » on the left (notebook for a sheet or
  session, the session for an exercise opened from it, Reelbook for a notebook, Settings, the setup guide), the page
  title in the middle once its h1 has gone under the bar, « ⋯ » on the right on sheet and session pages.
- « ⋯ » opens the page's actions in a bottom sheet (L18): Move, Add to session, Edit, Keep screen on (switches in
  place, On / Off), Something wrong?, Delete sheet / Delete session. The buttons keep their ids (`mv-open`,
  `as-open`, `ed-open`, `wake`, `sd-open`); the page no longer carries them.
- Back: `history.back()` when the entry before is the parent, else `location.replace(parent)`. Each route keeps its
  scroll position, put back when the page is shown again from history or by Back.
- Leaving a session edit with unsaved changes (Back or a swipe) asks « Discard your changes? »; Cancel keeps the
  edit (a swipe is undone with history.forward / back, without redrawing the page).
- « + Add a video » is a round floating button (56 px, above the home indicator) on home and notebook pages; on a
  notebook the form says « It goes to <notebook> ». The header button and the crumbs (« ← Reelbook ») are gone.

## Why
Audit item E7: navigation that behaves like a native app (decision 7: no bottom tab bar).

## Decisions
- Menu items a page cannot do stay listed, greyed, with the reason: « Keep screen on — Not available in this
  browser » (no Wake Lock API), « Something wrong? — No source video ». The L17 acceptance test asks for both on a
  sheet without source in a harness without the API; before, they were hidden (L3, L7).
- « Something wrong? » stays under the notes too (L7 acceptance); the menu's item is `#nav-report` (no duplicate id).
- A sheet opened from a session keeps « ← <session> » above its dose banner (L5 acceptance), next to the bar's
  « ‹ <session> ».
- The floating button shows « + » only (round, as the spec says); its text « + Add a video » stays for screen
  readers (aria-label « Add a video »), font-size 0.
- Notebook pages and Settings have no « ⋯ » (no page action there).
- While the catalogue loads or cannot be read, a route other than home's is taken for a notebook (bar shown, its
  title from the library or the route).

## Pitfalls
- `tests/harness.cjs` keeps one listener per event type: `openSheet` wiring a dialog's backdrop click replaced the
  menu's own click handler. `wireSheet(dlg)` now wires a sheet ahead of its own handlers.
- The acceptance tests read labels as `textContent` (the harness does not parse the markup): the app sets « ⋯ » and
  « + Add a video » once at start.
- L13's acceptance test contradicted L17's (open question in COORDINATION): settled, see Arbitrage.
