## What
- **Keep screen on**: a toggle in the top bar of sheet and session pages, next to Move / Edit. On, the page holds
  a screen wake lock; the choice is remembered on the device (`rb.wake`), asked again when the page comes back to
  the front, released when leaving sheet and session pages or when turned off. No button when the browser has no
  Screen Wake Lock API.
- **Gesture first**: a sheet built from `templates/sheet.html` now reads header → sequence with photos → dose →
  three key points (the others behind « +N more ») → why, collapsed under its own heading (« Pourquoi ») → the rest
  in its original order. Done at display time (`app/sheet.js`), sheets are not regenerated. Exercise sheets
  (`templates/exercise.html`) were already in that order and are unchanged.
- **Full-screen photos**: tapping (or Enter on) a step photo opens a black viewer with all the step photos of the
  page side by side (scroll snap), the tapped one first, the step title and text under each. ✕ (48 px) or Escape
  closes it and focus goes back to the photo.
- **img/thumb.jpg 404 fixed**: `loadSheet` imported the sheet into the page before rewriting photo URLs, so each
  `<img>` fetched its raw relative src once. URLs are now signed in the parsed (inert) document.

## Why
Audit items Q1, Q3, Q4: at the gym the phone lies on the floor; the screen locked after 30–60 s and the first
photo of the movement was 2.2–2.8 screens down. Now the first step photo is on the first screen.

## Decisions
- The DOM work lives in a new `app/sheet.js` (same pattern as `sessions.js`) so it can be unit-tested; the vm
  harness does not load it, and `loadSheet` skips it when absent. On any error the sheet shows as written (the body
  is restored from a copy).
- Blocks are found by structure, not by heading text: units = an `<h2>` and what follows up to the next one;
  sequence = first unit with `.steps` and an image; dose = first with `.dose`; key points = first with `ol.keys`;
  why = a unit of the header box placed before all three. Not found → left in place.
- Only the first sequence moves up; a second `.steps` section (variation, « L'exercice à terre », the second
  « La séquence » of foil-tack-kitesurf) stays with the rest.
- The why's `<h2>` goes inside the `<summary>` (allowed by HTML), so the heading is kept.
- The viewer shows every step photo of the page, variation included.
- The top bar may wrap to two lines on narrow screens (session pages: back link on the first line).
- The toggle is not shown in session edit mode (no buttons there); the lock stays as set.

## Pitfalls
- `tests/harness.cjs` keeps one listener per event type per element: a second `view.addEventListener('click')`
  would replace the app's; the toggle and the photos go through the existing click handler.
- `tests/mini-dom.cjs` is a small DOM for the unit tests (no jsdom in the repo): descendant selectors only, no `>`.
- `harness.cjs` gained a `nav` option (the navigator), for the wake-lock tests.
