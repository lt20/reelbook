## What
New sheets written by the skill follow stricter rules (existing sheets are not reprocessed, decision 6):
- One label per function on both templates: Why, Dose, Key points, Steps, Common mistakes, Easier / harder, with a
  French column in the skill (Pourquoi, Dose, Points clés, Étapes, Erreurs fréquentes, Plus facile / plus dur).
- The wrong movement goes in « Common mistakes » after Steps, as `.step.bad` (no number); the app draws a danger
  border and a ✕ badge (`/* L15 */` CSS block in `app/index.html`, the only app change).
- Eyebrow: fine target and duration only (« Biceps · 5 min »). Key points: 3 most important first, 8 at most.
- No « closest notebook »: no fit → `rb.py error <id> --note "No notebook fits: add <suggestion>?"`.
- Production notes go in the footer, not under Why.
- `duration` = practice time or `""`; optional `steps` (whole number). `rb.py publish` refuses a duration that looks
  like a video length (`0:45`) or a step count (`5 étapes`), and a `steps` that is not a whole number >= 1.

## Why
Audit item E5: labels varied between templates and sheets, mistakes sat in the numbered sequence, eyebrows repeated
the notebook and group, durations held video lengths or step counts, and a pizza was filed in Strength › Cooking.

## Decisions
- Exercise sheets now title their cues « Key points » (one label per function) and keep 2–3; the skill says the
  session still carries Why, Dose and the whole-session key points.
- A mistake has no number badge; its photo `alt` starts with « Mistake: ». A text-only mistake gets a ✕ before its
  title (`.step.bad>h3:first-child::before`).
- `steps` is checked by `rb.py` but not published: there is no column (a migration is out of this lot).
- `app/sheet.js` is unchanged: the reorder already treats « Common mistakes » as « the rest » (after Why), and the
  sequence is the first `.steps` block with photos. Tests prove it with the new templates in English and French.
- Fixtures: `pompes-inclinees` (sheet) and `session-gainage-planche` (exercise) follow the L15 templates; the other
  fixtures keep the older titles, like Laurent's existing sheets.

## Pitfalls
- The session player (L10) shows every `.step img` of the exercise in its carousel, so a mistake photo shows up
  there with no marker (measured: Planche has 3 dots, the third is « Erreur : Fesses en l'air »). For L11: skip
  `ph.img.closest('.step.bad')` photos in `playMedia`, or mark them.
- `rb-*.test.py` files are not found by `unittest discover` (dash in the name): run each file directly, as CI does.
