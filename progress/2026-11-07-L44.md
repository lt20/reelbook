## Open: `service_role` and the `add-link` Edge Function
Measured on the same fresh stack: `service_role` has `----` on every table. `capture_tokens` read and update, `requests` insert: 403 `42501`. So `add-link` (`supabase/functions/add-link/core.ts`) fails on a brand-new project. The brief limits this lot to `authenticated` (« Rien d'autre ne change »), so it is not in the file. The two lines it would take, untested with the function itself (the function was not served):

    grant select, update on public.capture_tokens to service_role;
    grant insert on public.requests to service_role;

Decision for Laurent, recorded in the PR.

## Choices
- `practice_log` `select, insert` and `user_themes` `select, insert, delete`: no `update` or `delete` call for them in `app/` or `tools/rb.py`. The policies stay `for all`: the grant is the narrower layer. An older project keeps its wider rights, nothing is revoked.
- Grants at the end of the file: every table exists by then, and the `revoke` lines on the beta tables are not contradicted since no grant names them.
- `touch_updated_at()` is executable by `anon` on the fresh stack (no `revoke` in the file, pre-existing). A trigger function cannot be called through the API; left alone.
