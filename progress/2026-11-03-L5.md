## What
- **Moving through a session**: an exercise card on a session page opens `#session/<slug>/<n>` (n counted from 1
  over the items whose sheet still exists). That page is the sheet with, on top, a banner « 2/4 · 3 × 20 s » (no
  « · » when the dose is empty) and a back link « ← <session title> »; at the bottom, after My notes,
  « ← Previous » and « Next: <next title> → » (56 px high). The first exercise has no Previous; the last shows
  « Back to the session ». A reload keeps all this; an out-of-range n opens the session page; `#<sheet>` is unchanged.
  « From the session … » leaves out the session the sheet is opened from. Keep screen on stays on between exercises
  (one wake lock, never released). Next scrolls back to the top.
- **Top bar of a sheet**: the group reads « Group · Back », normal case, muted, 14 px like the back link.
- **Notes save themselves** (sheet and session): 800 ms after the last keystroke, and at once on page hidden,
  pagehide and route change. « Saving… » / « Saved » / « Not saved » + Retry. No Save button. Unchanged text is
  never written. A failed save keeps the text in the field; Retry or the next keystroke saves again.
- **Skill**: a dose that the session format gives for every exercise is copied into each `items[].dose`.
- `RS.sessionSlugOf` reads `session/<slug>/<n>` too and refuses `…/0`, `…/nope`, `…/2/extra`; new
  `RS.sessionItemOf`, `RS.sessionItemHref`.

## Why
Audit Q5: an exercise opened from a session lost the session (back link to the notebook, no next, no dose).
Audit Q2: notes needed a Save tap, easy to forget mid-workout.

## Decisions
- Previous / Next sit after My notes and before « Delete sheet » (« at the bottom »): do the exercise, jot a note,
  Next. À confirmer.
- The status before any typing stays as it was: « No note yet. » / « Saved <date> ». « Saving… » shows from the
  first keystroke, during the 800 ms wait.
- Writes are queued one after the other, so an older text never lands after a newer one. A save still on its way is
  awaited before the note is read again (Previous right after typing shows the new text). A failed text is kept
  in memory for the session of the app and shown again with « Not saved » if the page is opened again.
- The textarea is read-only until the stored note is read, so a keystroke is never overwritten by the late read.
  A read error (`error` in the answer, which was ignored) now shows « Could not read the note. ».
- A slow sheet load that finishes after the user tapped Next again no longer overwrites the newer page.
- CSS in its own « L5 » block; no change to `card`, `.btn.sm`, `.mv` or colour tokens (L4).

## Pitfalls
- The vm harness runs `setTimeout` as a microtask and has no `clearTimeout`: the note timer uses a keystroke
  counter instead of `clearTimeout`. The 800 ms delay, visibilitychange and pagehide are checked in Chromium only.
- `page.element('sheet-top')` in the harness is not the `#sheet-top` inside `#view` (see Open questions in
  COORDINATION.md).
- `.top span` sets 12 px: `font-size:inherit` on the group label inherits 12 px from `.top .r`, hence 14 px.
