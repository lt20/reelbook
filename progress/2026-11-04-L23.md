## What
- The search view of the home page gets a row of facet chips under the field: Zone, Gear, Time, Type. Each opens a
  bottom sheet with the values present in the catalogue (all notebooks) and how many sheets hold each; ticked values
  combine (AND across facets, OR inside one) with the text query; results are the photo cards with their notebook.
  The choice lives in the hash (`#search?zone=shoulders,back&time=%3C%205`): Back restores the previous one.
- Schema: `sheets.tags jsonb not null default '{}'` + GIN index (additive, idempotent). **db push by Laurent.**
- `rb.py publish` checks `meta.json` `tags` against `facets` in `app/config.js` (unknown value: refused on a new
  sheet, warning on an update; malformed: always refused), sends them, retries without the column on an old backend.
- `rb.py backfill-tags [--dry-run]` derives tags without Claude (group → zone, keywords of title/search text → gear
  and type, duration → minutes) for sheets that have none. **Laurent runs it** after the db push.
- Skill: one rule per facet for `meta.json`.

## Why
O7: Laurent wants « shoulders · band · under 10 min » across all notebooks; until now only free text (L14).

## Decisions
- Chips only on the home page (all notebooks), not on a notebook page; shown once at least one sheet has tags
  (so hidden on a backend without the column and before the backfill). À confirmer.
- Counts are over the whole catalogue, not narrowed by the other facets or the text. À confirmer.
- Sessions have no tags: they leave the results as soon as a facet is chosen.
- One history entry per opening of a facet sheet (the next ticks replace it): Back undoes the sheet, not each tick.
- No nav bar on `#search?…` (it is the home page); a « Clear » button in the sheet empties one facet.
- `rb.py` reads the vocabulary from `app/config.js` (one source); no vocabulary readable → no unknown-value check.
- `publish` without `tags` in meta.json sends no `tags` (an update keeps the ones the sheet had); `minutes` defaults
  to the number in `duration`. The backfill skips a sheet when nothing can be derived (stays `{}`).
- Fixtures: curated tags on 27 of 30 synthetic sheets (jab-cross and the shadow-boxing sheets stay untagged).
- `config.js?v=7` + `rb-shell-35` (protocol: every module change bumps); L21 also bumps: one-line conflict expected.

## Pitfalls
- The harness's `window.REELBOOK` has no `facets`: the app must work without the vocabulary (time buckets fall back
  to the same values as config.js; order then alphabetical).
- Facet values go raw in `data-facet-value` (only `&` and `"` escaped): the acceptance test matches `< 5` there.
- The worktree guard refuses chained shell commands with variables or heredocs: patch via a script in the scratchpad.
