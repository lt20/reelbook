## What
Notebook page: sticky group chips with counts, one horizontal snap row of cards per group (standalone sheets, then
« From sessions »), « See all N » opening `#<notebook>~<group>` as a grid, sessions row on top, header
« N sheets · N sessions (N exercises) ». Cards everywhere: 4:5 photo framed on the upper third, 2-line summary, at
most 2 single-line pills. Preview fixtures: 25 Strength sheets.

Height of Strength on an iPhone 14: synthetic 9.0 → 5.5 screens; Laurent's 78 sheets (`--work`) 25 → 7.9 screens.

## Why
Audit E1 (a 26-screen grid) and E2 (cropped 9:16 frames, uneven cards).

## Decisions
- `#<notebook>~<group>` rather than a local toggle: the back button works, and back from a grid reopens the rows at
  that group. Old `#<slug>~<anchor>` links still open home (the part before `~` is not a notebook id).
- « See all » from 3 sheets; chips hidden under 2 groups; grid keeps the notebook title, group as section heading.
- Pills, most useful first: done · in N sessions · note · duration · author (sessions: N exercises first).
- The ≤ 5 screens target is not reachable with 13 rows of the spec's card (~390 px each): reported, not forced.
- Active chip: the group under the bar while scrolling, the last group on screen at the page bottom; a tapped chip
  stays active until the user scrolls (wheel / touchmove / keydown) or navigates. Row titles clamped to 2 lines.

## Pitfalls
- `class="dose"` on a card pill also matched the sheet's `.dose{display:grid;margin…}` block: the pill rule now sets
  `display:block;margin:0`.
- The acceptance test reads the **first** `.card img{` / `.card p{` rule in the file: card rules were edited in
  place, new rules live in the « L12 » block.
- The vm harness has no layout: the active-chip logic is checked by a Playwright script, not a unit test.
- Extending the fixtures changes the home counts: `tests/e2e/steps/l6.json` was updated.
