## What
`#setup` shows the two hosting cards of the landing. A full or closed beta turns the hosted card into the waiting list, on the
landing and in `#setup`, with own Supabase picked. The hosted sign-up form no longer offers own Supabase. A hosted sign-up reads
`beta_seats()` first, and again after « Database error saving new user », so each refusal has its message and the last seat taken
meanwhile leads to the list. Without `beta_seats`, nothing changes.

## Why
Decision 26: Laurent turns on the 50-seat cap when this is merged. The site has no cap today.

See « Decisions where the spec left a gap » and « À confirmer » in PR #85.

## Pitfalls
- The browser's locale follows the machine: the first « EN » captures were in French. Steps click the language explicitly.
- `.choice button` still applied to the waiting-list button inside the card. Those rules are gone: nothing in `.choice` is a card button any more.
- `label` is `font-weight:600` globally: the landing's radio cards read bold. `#intro label.lp-opt` resets it.
- `tests/acceptance/l25` asserts the blurb of `config.js` in `hosted-blurb`, so `#setup` keeps it and adds the limits beside it.
- `tools/preview/fake-supabase.js` answers `?seats=<n>|closed|last`, for L41 as well.
