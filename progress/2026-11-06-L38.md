## What
`#demo` now looks like an account. Home: the brand line, the counter, the catalogue's intro, the notebooks as the
real `.tcard` cards (band, round pictogram, counter, photos; colour and pictogram from `config.js` by notebook id),
then the sessions as a row of session cards. A notebook card opens `#demo/notebook/<id>`, the real notebook page:
eyebrow, cover band with the pictogram and the large title, counter, group chips (two groups or more), the Sessions
row, one row of photo cards per group (« in 1 session » tag as in the app). A sheet opened from the session shows
« 2/3 · 8 reps » and Previous / Next like the app; otherwise its way back is « ‹ <notebook> ». The banner is in
French when the landing's language is French (`window.RBL`, keys `demo.up` / `demo.back` added to the same
dictionary in `landing.js`).

## Why
Laurent, 6 Nov: the flat English grid was « vraiment dégueulasse »; a visitor should see the app he will get.

## Decisions
- The `.tcard` markup is shared: `tcard()` extracted from `renderPortal` (one call point outside the demo block).
- Photos on the cards keep the app's 4:5 ratio (the brief said 3:2, which is the landing v3 drawing in L36): the demo
  follows the real app. À confirmer.
- Only what the real pages scope by id (`#brand-wordmark`, `#count`, `#home.nb`, `#groups`) got `.demo` twins; the rest
  is the existing CSS. The unused `.demo-nb` rules went.
- A notebook with no sheet and no session in the catalogue is not shown (as before). No new English text: every
  label is one the app already uses (« Find it fast, when you need it », « in 1 session », « ← Previous »…).

## Pitfalls
- `landing.js` changed: its `?v=` goes to 2 in `index.html` and `sw.js` with `VERSION` (L36 edits the same lines:
  merge = highest + 1).
- `git checkout -b … origin/main` tracks `origin/main`: a bare `git push -u origin <branch>` tried to push to main
  (refused by protection). Push with `HEAD:refs/heads/<branch>`.
- On the fake backend, the real account pages are 16 px wider than a 390 / 1440 viewport (`scrollWidth`): the demo
  pages are not; not touched here (outside the lot).
