## What
`rb.py backfill-tags` derives better tags: type from the slug (a session's part included), then the notebook's
profile (technique / partner → skill), then the notebook (strength, yoga → stretch); no zone on technique / partner
sheets; a group that is not body areas (« Kicks & knees ») never gives a zone; title before group, triceps before
push-ups, push-ups → chest; gear values `plate`, `cable`, `machine`, `bench`, gear read from the slug too.

## Why
Laurent's dry run of 4 Nov (110 sheets): gear empty on 104, type on ~98, zones on 5 fight / foil sheets. On the
simulated table (same 110 slugs): gear empty 104 → 92, type empty 82 → 1, zones on technique / partner 5 → 0.

## Decisions
- Type order: title / group / slug keyword, profile, search text keyword, notebook. The profile comes before the
  search text so a kite sheet that mentions a « warm-up » stays a skill.
- Zone order: title body area, title movement (`ZONE_MOVES`), group (only if wholly body areas), slug.
- Notebook fallback limited to the spec's two (strength, yoga).

## Pitfalls
- The worktree guard refuses `for f in …; python3 $f`: run each test file on its own line.
- `_words` treats `-` as part of a word: the slug is matched with hyphens turned into spaces.
- Gear stays thin on slugs and titles alone; the real search text carries most of it (backfill-search first).
