## What
- Text follows the system text size on iPhone and iPad (Settings › Display › Text Size), in Safari and in the installed
  app: the root takes `font: -apple-system-body` (17 px at the default setting) and every font size is in `rem`.
- One type scale of 7 steps (`--fs-xs` 13 px … `--fs-2xl` 36 px at a 16 px root) + `--fs-display` for page titles.
  Section titles (h2) are lower: 22 px instead of 30 px. The condensed face stays on titles, numbers and doses.
- Tokens for radii (`--r-sm/md/lg/pill`) and spacing (`--s-1` … `--s-6`) replace the hard values that match.
- Card and step grids put as many 9.5rem columns as fit: two on a phone, one when the text is large.
- The wordmark is an SVG (`app/icons/reelbook-wordmark.svg`, `-light.svg` for dark), on the sign-in and setup screens
  and in the home bar, instead of « Helvetica Neue » text (Arial on Android and Windows).
- `--brand-coral` (the fold of the mark). The Strength colour `--p0` is now olive, no longer the accent blue. A notebook
  page shows its colour: a 6 px band over the eyebrow, the eyebrow in that colour.

## Why
Audit item E10: fixed px sizes ignored the iOS text size, 15 font sizes and as many radii had drifted apart, the
wordmark depended on a font most devices lack, and the first notebook had the same blue as every button.

## Decisions
- rem base 16 px (browser default). On iOS the root is the Dynamic Type body size, 17 px by default: every size is then
  17/16 of the desktop one (body 17 px as before, the rest about 6 % larger than before on iPhone).
- The keyword applies only under `@supports (-webkit-touch-callout:none)` (iOS / iPadOS): measured in Playwright WebKit
  26.6 on macOS, `html{font:-apple-system-body}` gives a 13 px root, the whole app a fifth smaller in desktop Safari.
  Chromium ignores the keyword (16 px, measured).
- Mapping of the old sizes: 12–13 → xs, 14 → sm, 15–17 → base, 18–21 → md (20), 22–24 → lg (22), 28–30 → xl (28),
  34–40 → 2xl (36). Plan: the PR description.
- Kept in px because an acceptance test reads the px value: `#count` 13 px, `#brand-wordmark` 22 px
  (tests/acceptance/l13), `.pl-dose` 64 px (tests/acceptance/l10). Tap targets, borders and the bottom-sheet radius
  `14px 14px 0 0` (tests/bottom-sheets) stay px by nature. Radii 6/10/14 px have no token (spec: replace where they
  match).
- Grids: 9.5rem, not the spec's 10.5rem (Codex pass 1): at 10.5rem a 375 px iPhone (343 px of content) and, with the
  17 px iOS root, a 390 px one fell to one column at the default text size. 9.5rem keeps two columns there (2 × 161.5 +
  12 ≤ 343) and gives one column from the next text size up (19 px), and at 130 % (measured at 320, 375, 390, 430 px; 320 px
  now has one column).
- Inputs: `font-size:max(16px,1rem)`, so a smaller iOS text size never makes Safari zoom into a field.
- Wordmark: « reelbook » outlined from Barlow Condensed Bold (OFL, the display face already hosted), tracking −18/1000;
  light variant in today's dark-mode wordmark colour #7fa6ea. The home `<h1>` keeps the text « Reelbook » (tests, screen
  readers), shown as the SVG by CSS.
- `--p0` light #5f7d1a (4.54:1 on --paper), dark #a9c45c (8.25:1). Olive is the largest free gap on the hue wheel of the
  palette. The notebook eyebrow mixes the colour 75 % with the ink so small text keeps ≥ 4.5:1 with every palette colour.
- Notebook page title: `overflow:visible` (the « g » of « Strength » was clipped by line-height .95, before L20 too) +
  `overflow-wrap:anywhere` (Codex pass 2: a long unbroken title overflowed; measured: wraps within 390 px).

## Pitfalls
- `-apple-system-body` is not iOS-only in WebKit: guard it.
- The 130 % check is `html{font-size:130%}` injected into a copy of the preview (Chromium has no Dynamic Type).
