# L10 — Session player: one exercise per screen (S1)

The bet three experts made independently: a session is played, not read. Builds on L5 (`#session/<slug>/<n>`), L3
(keep screen on, photo viewer) and L9 (structured dose).

## Route and entry
- A primary button « Start » on the session page (top, under the title) opens `#session/<slug>/play` (exercise 1),
  `#session/<slug>/play/<n>` for exercise n. A single sheet can be played too: « Start » on a sheet page opens
  `#<slug>/play` (one exercise, no previous/next).
- Progress (current exercise) remembered per user and session in `localStorage` (`rb.play.<uid>`); reopening
  `#session/<slug>/play` offers « Resume at 3/5 » or « Start over ».

## Screen (full screen, no top bar of the app; a close « ✕ » top-left ≥ 44 px back to the session page)
- Top: « 2/5 », the exercise title.
- The sheet's step photos in a horizontal `scroll-snap` carousel, full width, 4:5, dots under it; tapping a photo
  opens L3's viewer.
- The dose very large (display font, ≥ 56 px): from L9 (« 3 × 10 », « 40 s », « 10 / side »), else the free text, else
  nothing.
- The first 3 key points of the sheet (same extraction as L3's `app/sheet.js`).
- Bottom, in the thumb zone: « ← Previous » and « Next → » buttons ≥ 64 px high, full width split; on the last
  exercise « Finish » (goes to the session page for now; L11 adds the end screen).
- Keep screen on is **on** while playing, whatever the toggle (released on leaving the player).
- Swipe left/right on the dose/key points area = next/previous (not on the carousel).
- Dark background by default in the player (readable in a gym), respects light/dark tokens otherwise.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: no animated transitions.
