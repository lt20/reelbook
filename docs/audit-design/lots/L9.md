# L9 — Structured dose (S2)

Why: the session player (L10, L11) needs sets, reps, time and rest as numbers. Today `items[].dose` is free text
(« 3×10 », « 30 s », « 1 min de chaque côté », « 3 × 8 · 4 s ↓ ») and the session `format` carries the rest
(« 60 à 90 s de repos »).

## Parser (pure, in `app/sessions.js`, exported on `RS`)
- `RS.parseDose(text) → {sets, reps, seconds, perSide, raw}` (each field `null` when absent; `raw` = trimmed input).
  Must read at least: `3×10`, `3 x 10`, `3 × 8-12` (reps = upper bound 12, keep `repsMin` 8), `30 s`, `40 sec`,
  `1 min`, `1 min 30`, `2'30`, `3 × 30 s`, `10 de chaque côté` / `each side` / `par côté` / `per side` (perSide true),
  `5 rounds`, French and English. Anything else → all null, `raw` kept. Never throws.
- `RS.parseRest(text) → seconds | null` from a session format: « 60 à 90 s de repos » → 90 (upper bound),
  « repos 1 min » → 60, « 20 s rest » → 20, « no rest » / « sans repos » → 0.
